## Scope

Whole site (home, projects, project pages, About, 404), led by the home page. Visitor mode: Persuade. Audience: recruiters and hiring managers (remote abroad, Lagos companies) and freelance clients, often on phones. Action: email Harrison or download the CV. Proof: the shipped products and his role in each. Constraints: professional first; Black Clover visible but tasteful (no official artwork, character art only when uploaded by Harrison with rights); no glassmorphism; no visible location (it stays in metadata for search); everything the admin edits stays data-driven.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Harrison's work rides a route. The site is a bus livery and its route board, repainted in cement grey and burgundy: products are stops, roles are painted on a burgundy band. It refuses the dark developer portfolio with a code card and a grid of equal project cards.

OWN-WORLD: Cement-grey body (#d4d0c9), burgundy bands (#6d1526) for roles, strips and calls to action, warm-white signboards with a double frame for reading, a charcoal route board with lit and ghosted stops. One typeface, Bricolage Grotesque: 800 for display, narrow caps for labels. Screenshots sit in dark bus windows. A black five-leaf clover decal; a manga panel (speed lines, speech bubble, black grimoire) before Contact. Night service: charcoal body, the same burgundy bands, and charcoal signboards (#201e1e), because warm-white boards glare on a dark screen at night.

STORY: A visitor sees a developer who ships whole products, reads his roles in one band, looks at real screenshots, and emails him or takes the CV.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Charcoal route board (name, stops, Hire me). On grey: "Harrison James" large in Bricolage 800, "Full-stack web developer.", the intro, then Email me (burgundy) and Download CV (clover icon). His photo in a dark window at the right with the sticker "Surpass your limits. Right here, right now." and a black clover decal. Full-bleed burgundy band at the bottom carrying the roles as links.

FORM: Lagos danfo livery and signwriting, candidate 6 of 7, seed key 5554832b, translated at Harrison's request: palette from danfo yellow to grey and burgundy, Bowlby One signwriting to Bricolage Grotesque, the rolling destination blind removed. Signature moment: the "Off the clock" manga panel, speed lines converging on a floating black grimoire.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
