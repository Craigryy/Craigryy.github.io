An investing app for people who want one place to hold dollars, follow the market and put money into plans.

## What it does

- **A landing page** that explains the product: live crypto prices, investment plans, security and how it works in three steps.
- **Sign-up and login**, including login with a fingerprint.
- **A dashboard** with the account balance, available to withdraw, total deposited, unrealised P&L and the latest trades.
- **Deposits in US dollars** by bank transfer, debit card or USDT, with quick amounts and clear instructions.
- **Trading and settings:** buy and sell, price charts, and profile and security settings.
