Owodex lets people turn gift cards into cash, or buy cards for services like Amazon, Apple, Steam, PlayStation and
Xbox. It is built to be quick to use on a phone.

## What it does

- **Sell gift cards** in a few taps, with a clear path from picking a brand to getting paid.
- **Buy gift cards** from local and international brands.
- **Popular brands** are shown as cards with the country and currency, so there is no guessing.
