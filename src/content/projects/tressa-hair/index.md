Tressa is a storefront for a hair brand that sells wigs and bundles. It is designed for the phone first, because
that is where most of its customers shop.

## What it does

- **Find your texture.** Shoppers filter the range by bone straight, body wave or bobs, and pick a length to see its price.
- **Best sellers.** Product cards show the grade, the length options and the price before you tap through.
- **Shoppable reels.** A swipeable "In motion" strip opens full-screen stories, each with a "Shop this look" button.
- **Order on WhatsApp.** A floating WhatsApp button keeps the shop one tap away.
- **Reviews, offers and delivery info.** A holiday countdown with a promo code, buyer reviews and same-day delivery notes.
