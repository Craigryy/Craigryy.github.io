A Christian publishing site, built and run by a team of two. I'm one of its two developers: I've designed pages, built
whole sections from the database up, and added many of the features readers use today.

## What I built

- **The events section and its calendar.** Event pages, a month calendar worked out on the server, and countdowns to
  what's coming up.
- **The resources library.** The models, URLs and page design, and the backend tests that cover them.
- **Page design** for several pages, including the welcome page visitors land on first.
- **Features across the site** that make it faster, more reliable, and installable on a phone like an app.
- **Deployment and tooling.** Moved the site off Heroku to Docker on DigitalOcean, and set up the tools the team works
  with every day.
